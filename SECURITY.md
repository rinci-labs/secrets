# Security

Report vulnerabilities by email to **hello@rin.ci** with "security" in the subject. Please do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version affected. You will get a reply from the person maintaining the code.

The threat model, what the tool protects against and what it does not, is documented at [secrets.rin.ci/docs/security](https://secrets.rin.ci/docs/security).
